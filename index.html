A little café, a gift shop, and the best stop on Walker Street.

01 — Eat

Made fresh, packed for the road or the table.

Travelers heading to Big Bend stop in for the brisket and chicken quesadillas; locals come back for the sandwiches, wraps and salads.

Grab-and-go fridge by the counter, a cozy spot to sit, and options for different diets — including vegan and kid-friendly picks.

02 — Shop

There is more here than food.

Past the café counter you'll find a vintage gift shop: one-of-a-kind souvenirs, greeting cards, Big Bend postcards, apparel — and a shelf of goods made in Boquillas, México.

  • Vintage & unique souvenirs
  • Cards & Big Bend postcards
  • Apparel & baskets
  • Made in Boquillas, México
